Setup & help

1. Import your fleet

In Excel, open the TRUCK MASTER sheet, select columns A to E (Truck, Plate, Brand, Model, Size), copy, and paste below. This replaces the current fleet.

2. Import downtime history

Open DOWNTIME DETAILS, select Date, Truck No, Cause, and Explanation, copy, and paste below with "Downtime" selected. Rows are added and exact duplicates are skipped.

Quick guide

  1. Home shows uptime for the month selected at the top, with charts, weekly totals, and top causes.
  2. Daily entry is for recording trucks that are down. Tap the box for that truck and day, then choose a cause.
  3. Downtime log lists every entry. Use it to change a cause or explanation, filter by cause, or delete a mistake.
  4. Uptime for a day = (trucks in fleet − trucks down) ÷ trucks in fleet × 100.

Full instructions are in the separate User Manual.
